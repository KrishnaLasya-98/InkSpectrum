## 📚 2. CORE LESSONS & ESSENTIAL CONCEPTS

### **Lesson 1A: The Beach Story (THEORY)**
- **Core Theme:** Family bonding, creativity, imagination, sharing
- **Characters:** Varun (boy), Vidya (girl, his sister), Mummy, Daddy, Grandpa, Grandma
- **Setting:** A sunny day at the beach with family
- **Central Activity:** Building an imaginary sandcastle with rooms for everyone
- **Key Vocabulary:** Cloud, Sandcastle, Sand, Palm Tree, Sea, Ball, Girl, Swimming Ring, Starfish, Shovel, Bucket, Basement, Staircase, Castle, Hide-and-seek, Playroom, Toys

### **Lesson 1B: Comprehension & Critical Thinking**
- Identifying the correct sentences from the story
- Distinguishing true statements from false
- Open-ended thinking (what/why/where questions)
- Personal reflection (feelings, introductions)

### **Lesson 1C: Grammar Foundation**
- Introduction to **Vowels** (a, e, i, o, u)
- Introduction to **Consonants** (the other 21 letters)
- Total of 26 letters in the English alphabet

### **Lesson 1D: Vocabulary & Word Formation**
- **Joined Words (Compound Words)**: sand+castle=sandcastle, stair+case=staircase, base+ball=baseball, air+plane=airplane, chalk+board=chalkboard, snow+man=snowman, hair+brush=hairbrush, butter+fly=butterfly
- **Phonics Focus**: Short /a/ sound words: mat, van, rat, dad, nap, at, have, sandcastle

### **Lesson 1E: Life Skills & Self-Assessment**
- Self-introduction template
- Listening carefully
- Family story time
- Self-reflection through smiley ratings

## 📝 4. VERBATIM TEXT SEGMENTS (MUST PRESERVE EXACTLY)

### **Story Dialogue (Page 18):**
```
"Hey, let us build a sandcastle."
"Sure! I have a shovel and a bucket too."
"Varun and Vidya build a castle together."
"Look, this room is for Grandpa and Grandma."
"Wow! They will tell us stories here."
"Varun and Vidya build a very big castle."
"This room is for Mummy and Daddy. And that one is our playroom."
"We can keep all our toys here."
"Where does this staircase go?"
"It goes to the basement. We can play hide-and-seek there. Nobody will find us there."
"Mom and Dad are calling. We have to leave now!"
```

### **Vocabulary Words (Page 17):**
```
Cloud, Sandcastle, Sand, Palm Tree, Sea, Ball, Girl, Swimming Ring, Starfish
```

### **Glossary Definitions (Page 19):**
```
shovel: a tool for digging earth
basement: rooms or wide space below the ground floor of a building
staircase: a set of stairs in a building
```

### **Self-Introduction Template (Page 21):**
```
"Hello, My name is _____. I am ______ years old. I live in _______. My birthday is on ____________."
```

### **Example Dialogue (Page 20):**
```
"Hello! I am Adithya."
"Hello! Adithya, I am Smitha."
"I live in Mumbai."
"I live in Delhi. I am in Grade-1."
```

### **Grammar Definitions (Page 21):**
```
"There are 26 letters in the English alphabets. These letters can be divided into vowels and consonants."
"The five letters a, e, i, o, and u in the red circles are called vowels."
"The rest of the twenty-one letters are called consonants."
```

### **Joined Words Rule (Page 22):**
```
"Words that are made up of two smaller words are called Joined words."
"sandcastle = sand + castle"
"staircase = stair + case"
```

### **Phonics Rule (Page 23):**
```
"mat, van, rat, dad, nap, at, have, sandcastle — These are all words having the short /a/ sound"
```

### **Self-Assessment Statements (Page 25):**
```
1. I can read and enjoy a text on 'At the beach'.
2. I can differentiate between vowels and consonants.
3. I can identify the joined words.
4. I can listen carefully and understand the words.
5. I know how to introduce myself.
```

## 🎙️ 7. AUDIO REQUIREMENTS

### **Teacher Voice Persona:**
- Warm, encouraging, patient primary school teacher
- Indian English accent (neutral, not regional)
- Clear pronunciation for phonics emphasis
- Pace: Slightly slower than conversational for Grade 1 comprehension

### **Recommended TTS Voice:**
- **Microsoft Edge TTS:** `en-IN-NeerjaNeural` (Indian English female) or `en-US-AriaNeural` (neutral female)

### **Background Music:**
- Soft, upbeat ukulele or piano loop at 20% volume
- Music swells during scene transitions
- Music ducks to 5% during teacher narration

### **Sound Effects:**
- Gentle wave sounds (during beach scenes)
- Soft "ding" or chime for answer reveals
- Page-turn whoosh for section transitions

Read and Enjoy

Let us read what Varun and his sister do when they visit a beach with their family. It is a lovely day. Varun and his sister Vidya are at a beach with their parents.

Hey, let us build a sandcastle. Sure! I have a shovel and a bucket too. Varun and Vidya build a castle together. Look, this room is for Grandpa and Grandma. Wow! They will tell us stories here. Varun and Vidya build a very big castle. This room is for Mummy and Daddy. And that one is our playroom. We can keep all our toys here. Where does this staircase go? It goes to the basement. We can play hide-and-seek there. Nobody will find us there. Mom and Dad are calling. We have to leave now!

Glossary shovel : a tool for digging earth. basement : rooms or wide space below the ground floor of a building staircase : a set of stairs in a building

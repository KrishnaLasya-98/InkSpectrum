Animals are living beings like us which are found in nature. Animals are found all over the world and they need food, shelter, air, and water like other living beings, to grow and survive. Their size, eating habits, body colour, and body covering vary according to the place they are found in. All animals need care and protection.

### Let’s Recall

- Animals live both on land and in water.
- Animals are of different sizes.
- Animals that can fly are called birds.
- Very small animals are called insects.
- Animals that can be kept at home are domestic animals.
- Animals that live in jungle are called wild animals.
- Different animals live in different homes and eat different foods.

## Glossary

- Birds : Animals that can fly are called birds.
- Insects : Very small animals are called insects.
- Domestic animals: animals that can be kept at home.
- Wild animals : Animals that live in jungle..
- Den : Place where lions live.

Animals are living beings like us which are found in nature. Animals are found all over the world and they need food, shelter, air, and water like other living beings, to grow and survive. Their size, eating habits, body colour, and body covering vary according to the place they are found in. All animals need care and protection.

Animals live both on land and in water.

Water animals.

Animals that live in water are called “Water Animals or Aquatic Animals”.

Land animals.

Animals that live on land are called “Land Animals”.

Lion Elephant Tiger

Birds

Birds are the animals with feathers, wings and two-legs and a beak without teeth.

Duck Pigeon Parrot

Insects

Very small animals are called “Insects”. They have six legs. Some insects have wings to fly.

Butterfly Housefly Grasshopper

Domestic Animals: Animals that are kept at home or around our homes are called “Domestic Animals”. They are useful to us.

Pet Animals: Some animals like dog, parrot and rabbit are kept as pets. We love them and take care of them.

Wild Animals : Some animals can be seen only in the forests. They are called “Wild Animals”.

Animal Homes :

Animals need home to live and food to grow. Different animals eat different kinds of food and live in different homes.

Lions :

  • • Lions live in caves in forests.

  • • These caves are called “Dens”.

  • • They eat flesh of other animals.

Rabbits :

  • • They are burrowing animals and live in burrows

  • • They eat vegetables.

    Birds :

  • • Birds build their own nest.

  • • They eat seeds, berries, fruit, insects, etc.

    Ants :

  • • They make a hill-like structure using sand called “Anthill”.

  • • Ants live in a group like bees.

Domestic and pet animals are kept in human-made homes. A dog lives in a kennel

A cow lives in a shed A horse lives in a stable
